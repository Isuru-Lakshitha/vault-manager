## Full description (max 4000)
Vault Manager is a private, simple way to see where your money goes and stay on top of what's coming.

ALL YOUR MONEY IN ONE PLACE
• Bank, cash, wallet and card accounts in any currency
• Net worth and "safe to spend" at a glance
• Fast transaction entry, categories, tags and split payments

STAY AHEAD OF BILLS AND DEBT
• Bills and recurring payments with reminders
• Credit cards: balances, payments and utilisation
• Loans and IOUs with interest and payoff tracking

PLAN AND SAVE
• Monthly budgets with 80% and 100% alerts
• Savings goals with contributions and withdrawals
• Reports and charts, plus a monthly close to check your balances

WORKS THE WAY YOU DO
• Opens instantly and works offline
• Start as a guest with no sign-up, then save to an account any time
• Same account on Android and the web, synced
• Import bank statements (OFX, QFX, QIF) and export CSV, Excel or PDF
• English, Sinhala and Tamil

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• PIN lock when the app opens, and privacy mode to hide balances
• Your data is only visible to your account; no ads, no selling data
• Encrypted backups, and you can delete your account and data in the app

Vault Manager is a personal finance tracker. It does not move money, connect to your bank, or give financial advice.
